The temperature sensor accepts readings from minus 20 to 60 degrees Celsius. Values outside this range are rejected.

The sensor reports temperature every 30 seconds while connected to external power.

On battery power the sensor reports once every five minutes.

The sensor stores up to 500 readings while offline. The oldest reading is replaced when storage is full.

An amber indicator means the battery is below 15 percent.

A red indicator means the temperature probe is disconnected.

The sensor uses two AA batteries. Rechargeable AA batteries are supported.

Factory reset removes stored readings and Wi-Fi credentials.

Hold the reset button for ten seconds to start a factory reset.

The calibration offset can be set between minus 3 and plus 3 degrees Celsius.

The enclosure is rated for indoor use. It must not be exposed to rain.

Each exported reading includes a UTC timestamp and a temperature in degrees Celsius.
