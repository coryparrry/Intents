Items marked fragile must be packed with foam inserts.

A parcel may weigh at most 20 kilograms for the standard courier service.

Parcels over 20 kilograms must use the freight service.

Orders received before 14:00 on a working day are dispatched that day.

Orders received at or after 14:00 are dispatched on the next working day.

A missing item blocks dispatch of the entire order until the discrepancy is resolved.

A supervisor must approve replacing an ordered item with an alternative.

Returned electronic items go to inspection before they can be restocked.

The packing slip contains item names and quantities but excludes payment-card details.

Damaged stock is placed in the quarantine area and cannot be allocated to orders.

The dispatch scan records the parcel identifier and courier tracking number.

A cancelled order must not be dispatched even if a shipping label has already been printed.
