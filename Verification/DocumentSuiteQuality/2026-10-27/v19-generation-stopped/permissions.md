Workspace exports are available to owners. Members can view reports but cannot export a workspace.

Guests may read published reports. Guests cannot edit report drafts.

An invitation link expires 48 hours after it is created.

A workspace may contain at most 25 active members.

Only an owner can delete a workspace. Deletion requires entering the workspace name.

Archived workspaces are read-only. Restoring a workspace makes it editable again.

Owners can rotate an API key. Rotating a key immediately revokes the previous key.

A failed sign-in is recorded in the audit log. Audit records are retained for 90 days.

Removing a member ends their workspace access immediately. Their existing reports remain in the workspace.

A workspace name must contain between 3 and 60 characters.

Changing the workspace time zone affects future scheduled reports, not reports already generated.

A transfer of ownership must be accepted by the new owner before the old owner loses that role.
